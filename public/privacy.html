KarishmaV2 Privacy

Updated 16 November 2026. This page describes the hosted MVP, not planned local AI or future task automation.

Google sign-in

Signing in shares your basic Google profile (name, email, profile picture and account identifier) with KarishmaV2. We use it to identify your account. No Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar access is requested. A signed, HttpOnly session cookie remembers sign-in for up to 30 days. Logout removes that cookie from this browser.

Chats and memory

Chat history and memory notes are saved in this browser's storage, not synced to a cloud account or other devices. They are not encrypted by the app. Signed-in accounts use separate local history and note storage; earlier guest data stays in its guest storage. People who can access this browser or device may be able to read stored data.

Cloud AI and hosting

This hosted demo uses Groq for AI replies. Messages, recent conversation context and enabled memory notes are sent through the app's server to Groq to generate a reply. Do not enter passwords or sensitive personal information. Vercel hosts the site and may process normal request metadata such as IP addresses. Google processes sign-in. Each service has its own privacy policy.

Your controls

You can use the guest option, turn off memory in chat, edit or remove notes, export browser data, or clear the site's stored data using browser settings. Logout is not a request to delete your local notes or history. Clearing browser data removes locally saved chats and notes; there is no cloud backup in this MVP.
